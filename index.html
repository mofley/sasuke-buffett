Entry Checklist

Static pre-entry review. The goal is not to find more trades; it is to reject weak trades faster.

  • Is there a real catalyst or clear reason this stock is moving?
  • Is volume meaningfully above normal, not just visually exciting?
  • Is liquidity sufficient for clean entry and exit?
  • Is the bid/ask spread acceptable relative to your stop?
  • Is the trend clear on the timeframe you are trading?
  • Are you entering near a logical level instead of chasing a stretched candle?
  • Is there confirmation after the move, not just hope that it keeps going?
  • Is your stop obvious before entry?
  • Is your target realistic based on structure, range, and resistance?
  • Is estimated risk/reward at least 1:2?
  • Does this fit your time window and ability to monitor the trade?
  • Are you calm enough to execute the stop without renegotiating?
  • Would you still take this trade if you could only take one trade today?
